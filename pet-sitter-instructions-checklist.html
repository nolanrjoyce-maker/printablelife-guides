What to Leave Your Pet Sitter: A Complete Checklist

A practical checklist of everything a pet or house sitter needs before you travel: contacts, feeding, medication, house notes and an emergency plan.

Contacts your sitter needs

  • Your mobile numbers and the best times to reach you, including any time-zone difference.
  • A nearby friend or neighbor who has a key and can make decisions if you can't be reached.
  • Your regular vet and the nearest 24-hour emergency vet, with addresses and hours.
  • A pet poison hotline. The ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center is (888) 426-4435 and may charge a consultation fee.

One profile per pet

  • Food brand, exact amount per meal, meals per day, and where the food is stored.
  • Treats that are allowed and foods that are never allowed.
  • Medications: dose, timing, whether to give with food, and how your pet takes pills.
  • Hiding spots, escape habits, fears, and what normal energy and appetite look like.
  • Microchip number and the company it is registered with.

A daily schedule

  • Write the day as morning, midday, evening and bedtime, with the task and amount for each pet.
  • For drop-in sitters, list the visit times and how long each visit should last.
  • Add the jobs that happen every few days: fresh water, full litter changes, cage or tank cleaning.

House notes

  • How to lock up and arm or disarm the alarm. Keep codes off any copy that leaves the house.
  • Wi-Fi, thermostat settings, trash and recycling day, mail and packages, plants to water.
  • Where the water shut-off, breaker panel, flashlights, first-aid kit and pet carriers are.

An emergency plan

  • Tell your sitter to call the vet right away for trouble breathing, collapse or seizures, heavy bleeding, repeated vomiting, a swollen hard belly, straining to urinate, or eating something toxic.
  • Write a short note authorizing your sitter to get veterinary care, with a spending limit. Ask your vet ahead whether they need it, a deposit, or a call from you.
  • Leave a recent photo of each pet in case one goes missing.

Before you leave

  • Refill medication and buy food and litter to last the trip plus three extra days.
  • Check that your pet's microchip registration has your current phone number.
  • Do a short trial visit so your pet meets the sitter while you're home.
  • Photograph every page you leave so you and the sitter both have a copy.
