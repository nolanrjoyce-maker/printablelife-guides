Buying a Used Car: Questions to Ask About Service History

What to look for in a seller's maintenance records, which gaps matter, and the questions to ask before you buy.

What a good record looks like

  • Dated receipts with odometer readings that increase steadily.
  • Oil changes at roughly the interval the owner's manual recommends.
  • Major scheduled services (timing belt, transmission fluid, brakes) done around the expected mileage.

Questions to ask

  • Who did the work: a shop, a dealer, or the owner? Can you see receipts?
  • Any accidents, recalls or warranty repairs?
  • Why are you selling, and what will need doing soon?

Gaps worth asking about

  • Long stretches with no service at all.
  • Odometer readings that go backwards or jump unexpectedly.
  • A major service that's overdue on the schedule.

Before you pay

  • Get an independent pre-purchase inspection.
  • Check a vehicle history report and open recalls for the VIN.
