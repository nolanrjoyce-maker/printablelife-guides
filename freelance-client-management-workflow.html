Freelance Client Management Workflow

A structured approach to organizing client interactions, tracking deliverables, and maintaining professional communication without relying on external tools.

Client Intake and Onboarding

  • Create a dedicated page for each new client containing their contact details, project scope, and agreed-upon deadlines.
  • Draft a standardized welcome message template that outlines communication channels, file submission methods, and payment terms.
  • Establish a checklist of initial requirements such as logo files, brand guidelines, or access credentials needed to begin work.

Task Breakdown and Tracking

  • Decompose large project milestones into smaller, actionable sub-tasks with specific due dates for accurate progress monitoring.
  • Assign status tags like 'To Do', 'In Progress', or 'Review' to each task to visualize current workflow stages.
  • Maintain a shared log of completed tasks and pending items to ensure both parties have visibility into project status.

Communication Centralization

  • Log all email exchanges and meeting notes in the client's page to create a single source of truth for context.
  • Use a recurring template for weekly update summaries that highlights progress, blockers, and next steps.
  • Set clear guidelines within the log indicating which messages require immediate response versus those awaiting client input.

Deliverable Management

  • Create a version control section listing file names, dates of modification, and revision numbers for every asset delivered.
  • Record feedback comments alongside specific assets to track changes without losing original context or historical data.
  • Maintain a final approval checklist confirming all deliverables meet the initial scope before marking the project complete.

Invoicing and Financial Records

  • Link invoice numbers directly to specific projects or client pages to correlate billing with completed work.
  • Track outstanding balances by recording payment dates, amounts due, and confirmed receipt status in a dedicated list.
  • Keep notes on agreed-upon scope changes that justify additional billing or contract amendments.

Project Closure and Retention

  • Draft a post-project summary highlighting achievements, lessons learned, and total hours billed for future reference.
  • Save final contact information and feedback in a client archive to facilitate smoother transitions if the relationship ends.
  • Identify opportunities for future collaboration by noting any pending needs or recurring maintenance requirements.
