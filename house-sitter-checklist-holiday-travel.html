House Sitter Checklist for Holiday Travel

A room-by-room list of what a house sitter needs to know when you travel over the holidays: heating, deliveries, decorations, storms and security.

Holiday-specific notes

  • Packages: expect more of them. Say where to put deliveries and whether to open any that need refrigeration.
  • Decorations and lights: which ones run on timers, which must be unplugged at night, and that candles stay unlit.
  • Trees and plants: watering instructions for a real tree, and that poinsettias, lilies and mistletoe stay out of pets' reach.
  • Guests: say clearly whether the sitter may have visitors.

Cold weather

  • Thermostat minimum to prevent frozen pipes, and where the water shut-off valve is.
  • Who clears snow from the walk and driveway, and where the shovel and ice melt are.
  • What to do in a power outage: flashlights, where the breaker panel is, and who to call.

Security

  • Which doors and windows to lock, how to arm the alarm, and which lights stay on.
  • A neighbor who knows the sitter is staying, so nobody calls the police on them.
  • Keep alarm codes and Wi-Fi passwords off any copy that leaves the house.

The basics

  • Trash and recycling days, which often shift around holidays.
  • Mail, plants and any pets: see the full pet-sitter checklist linked below.
